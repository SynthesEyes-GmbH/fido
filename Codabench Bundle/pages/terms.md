## Awards

| Award | Amount |
|---|---|
| Best performing team — Task 1 (Keypoint Detection) | t.b.a. |
| Best performing team — Task 2 (Registration) | t.b.a. |
| Most innovative/impactful approach | t.b.a. | 

The top-performing team per task is determined by the quantitative leaderboard. The most innovative/impactful approach is selected by the organizing committee. A single team may win multiple awards.

## Participation

- Submissions are fully automatic (no human-in-the-loop at inference).
- Teams from organizer institutes (SynthesEyes GmbH, Carl Zeiss AG/Meditec AG, TU Munich, Rotterdam Eye Hospital) may participate but are **not eligible for awards**.

## Data Usage

- The provided dataset may be used for research and non-commercial purposes only.
- Additional publicly available datasets and pre-trained models are permitted; any such use must be clearly documented in the method description.
- Private annotations of public data are allowed.

## Publication

- Organizers will coordinate a joint results paper within six months of the challenge.
- Each team nominates up to two main contributors as co-authors.
- Individual publication of results is permitted after the joint paper is published, with citation of the official FIDO Challenge manuscript.
