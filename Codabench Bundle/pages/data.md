# Data

## Dataset Overview

Fully synthetic multimodal retinal surgery data generated by **SynthesEyes GmbH**, modelled on clinical cases from the Rotterdam Ophthalmic Institute with imaging specifications provided by Carl Zeiss Meditec AG.

Each case contains synchronized:
- **Fundus microscope images** — 1024×1024 RGB, wide-field en-face view (ZEISS surgical microscope characteristics)
- **iOCT data** — high-resolution cross-sectional/volumetric imaging (ZEISS iOCT characteristics)


Annotations are generated directly from simulation ground truth and validated by experienced retina surgeons. License: **CC BY-NC-ND**.

## Task 1 — Keypoints & Tool–Tissue Distance

- 10 training videos / 5 test videos (hidden)
- ~1,000 frames per video at 15 fps (~1 min each)
- Per frame: fundus image + 2 iOCT cross-sections
- Annotations: tool keypoints (fundus + iOCT), tool–tissue distance, iOCT-to-fundus transform, eye pose

## Task 2 — iOCT-to-Fundus Registration

- 150 training / 30 validation / 30 test snapshots (hidden)
- Per snapshot: 1024×1024 fundus image + 512×256×256 iOCT volume (256 slices of 256×256)
- Annotations: ground-truth 3D-to-2D transformation, vessel segmentations, retinal layer masks

## Submission

Submissions are Docker containers evaluated on Codabench. The hidden test data resides on the evaluation worker. The `input_data.zip` and `reference_data.zip` in this bundle are placeholders.

Required files: `inference.py`, `model.pth`. Optional: `requirements.txt` (packages installed before inference). Extra files are permitted.

All submissions must run inference on a single NVIDIA GPU (<24 GB). Per-case inference time limits will be announced at dataset release.
